## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Narrative
This design system defines the digital footprint of a senior cross-border autonomous systems and enterprise AI operational leader. The aesthetic merges precision engineering with executive poise: global operations spanning Hong Kong, Kuala Lumpur, and Dubai; mission-critical autonomous fleet scaling (WeRide); and high-stakes partnerships with frontier model labs (Anthropic, Caldera). The tone is relentlessly competent, authoritative, understated, and fast.

### Visual Style Archetype
**Dark Precision Minimalism (Linear × Vercel hybrid)**. The interface operates on deep midnight slate canvases, razor-sharp 1px structural boundaries, translucent frosted glass overlays, and restrained yet vibrant chromatic accents. Rather than relying on heavy ornaments, visual hierarchy is established through meticulous typographic contrast, micro-interactions, directional glow gradients, and monospaced technical telemetry counters.

### Emotional Signature
- **Certainty & Reliability:** Communicates zero-fail deployment, robust field operations, and institutional reliability.
- **Global Velocity:** Echoes cross-time-zone deployment nodes and rapid enterprise rollouts.
- **Frontier Intelligence:** Reflects an insider’s mastery of generative AI infrastructure and autonomous mobility.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined, low-glare dark canvas with tactical focus points.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Base Canvas (`#0B1220`):** Void-deep navy-black foundational background.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#131B2E`):** Primary card, panel, and modal background.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1E293B`):** Interactive card hovers, elevated popovers, and table header rows.
- **Subtle Perimeter Border (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.8)`): 1px structural rules delineating data grids and container walls without visual clutter.

### Accents & Signifiers
- **Primary Tech Blue (`#2563EB`):** System primary. Used for primary CTAs, operational status tags, active tab states, and key navigational focus indicators.
- **Vibrant Milestone Orange (`#F97316`):** Kinetic secondary. Dedicated to key metrics, high-impact numerical statistics, executive breakthroughs, critical milestones, and overseas hub indicators.
- **Telemetry Cyan (`#38BDF8`):** Tertiary status indicator for active deployments, real-time node pings, and model latency data.

### Typographic Neutral Stack
- **Text High-Contrast Primary (`#F1F5F9`):** 95% brightness slate-white for headlines, core data points, and active labels.
- **Text Muted Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Balanced mid-slate for descriptive body copy, operational context, and metadata.
- **Text Subdued / Code (`#64748B`):** Lower-tier metadata, inactive breadcrumbs, and timestamp strings.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses **Inter** (paired seamlessly with **Noto Sans SC** for Simplified and Traditional Chinese operational copy) for all primary communication, supplemented by **JetBrains Mono** for technical delivery parameters, city code coordinates (HKG, KUL, DXB), and telemetry labels.

### Scaling & Readability Directives
- **Tight Letter Tracking on Headlines:** Tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) produces the signature Linear/Vercel condensed confidence on all titles above 24px.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** Chinese copy rendered via Noto Sans SC should default to `400` weight for standard reading and `600` weight for headlines, ensuring stroke weight parity with Inter.
- **Monospace Telemetry:** Reserve JetBrains Mono exclusively for operational metrics, coordinates, timeline stamps, and deployment status keys to establish an authentic systems-engineering tone.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid locked within a maximum content boundary of `1280px`. Gutters fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`), outer canvas margin at `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout. Gutters scale down to `1.25rem` (`20px`), margin sets to `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout. Single column for longform and timeline nodes. Margins collapse to `1.25rem` (`20px`), gutters to `1rem` (`16px`).

### Layout Philosophy
Density over vacant emptiness. Layouts must balance breathe-through negative space with dense, rich information pods: 2-column or 3-column asymmetric modules featuring operational heatmaps, delivery milestone timelines, and hub data side-by-side. Vertical layout rhythm strictly adheres to an 8px base grid.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional drop shadows in favor of ambient luminescence, backdrop blurs, and layered micro-borders.

### Glassmorphism & Translucency
- **Sticky Executive Header:** `background: rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.75)`, layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a bottom border of `1px solid rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.08)`.
- **Command Overlays & Menus:** `background: rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.

### Border Lighting & Edge Contrast
- **Base Edge:** Cards rely on a crisp, razor-thin `1px` outline using `#1E293B`.
- **Hover Luminescence:** Interactive elements transition their borders to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)` accompanied by a subtle internal top rim highlight (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).

### Atmospheric Glow
Milestone cards and primary CTAs utilize an ultra-diffused atmospheric glow behind surfaces: radial gradient masks using `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` or `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.12)` with `blur(48px)` to signal operational prominence without introducing harsh drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard to project engineering rigour:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Chips, Small Buttons):** `0.25rem` (`4px`) or `0.375rem` (`6px`).
- **Cards, Interactive Panels, Milestone Containers:** `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (`8px`).
- **Overlays, Floating Navigation Bars, Modals:** `rounded-xl` at `0.75rem` (`12px`).
- **Pill Exception:** Live status beacons, city hub tags (e.g., `[● DXB - Dubai]`), and categorical metadata utilize `rounded-full` for instantaneous glanceability against squared containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#2563EB` fill, white `#F1F5F9` medium typography, `rounded` (4px to 6px), height `40px`, padding `0 18px`. Hover: `#1D4ED8` with a smooth 150ms transition. Micro-glow effect on active state.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px solid #1E293B` border, text `#F1F5F9`. Hover: background `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.05)`, border `#334155`.
- **Milestone Highlight Button / Accent:** Solid `#F97316` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, utilized sparingly for critical portfolio conversion points (e.g., "Schedule Strategic Briefing").

### Metric & Milestone Cards
- **Container:** Background `#131B2E`, 1px perimeter `#1E293B`, rounded `8px`, padding `24px`.
- **Accent Indicator:** Top-left or top-right indicator dot or badge in `#F97316` or `#38BDF8`.
- **Metric Value:** Displayed using `metric-display` (`48px`) in `#F1F5F9`, immediately followed by a descriptive label in `body-sm` (`#94A3B8`).

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Global Hub Chip:** High-density, monospaced metadata badge. Height `24px`, background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)`, border `1px solid rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.1)`, rounded `9999px`. Text: JetBrains Mono `11px`, `#F1F5F9`. Features a pulsing circular SVG beacon (e.g., green `#10B981` or tech blue `#2563EB`) representing active base operations.

### Deployment Timeline & Lists
- **Timeline Rails:** Vertical 1px line in `#1E293B`.
- **Milestone Nodes:** Square or circular nodes with `background: #0B1220` and `border: 2px solid #2563EB` (or `#F97316` for key institutional breakthroughs).
- **List Items:** Separated by `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.5)` with zero zebra-striping. Hover states introduce an inset highlight of `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Input:** Background `#0B1220`, border `1px solid #1E293B`, height `42px`, padding `0 14px`, text `#F1F5F9`, placeholder `#64748B`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#2563EB`, box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #2563EB`. No generic browser focus rings.

### Specialized Component: Interactive Global Fleet & Model Deployment Map
- Dark cartographic SVG canvas with subtle grid lines in `#1E293B`. Hub nodes (`HKG`, `KUL`, `DXB`, `SFO`) connected by bezier arcs colored in `#2563EB` with moving light-pulse accents (`#F97316`).
